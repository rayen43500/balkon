# MASTER PROMPT — BALKON L'AOUINA | PREMIUM RESTAURANT WEBSITE

Act as an elite digital creative agency combining the expertise of an award-winning UI/UX designer, senior frontend engineer, brand strategist, motion designer and luxury hospitality marketing specialist.

Your mission is to design and develop an extraordinary, premium, visually stunning and production-ready website for:

**BRAND: BALKON**
**LOCATION: L'Aouina, Tunis, Tunisia**
**INDUSTRY: Restaurant / Café / Lounge / Hospitality**

The result must feel like a world-class hospitality brand, not a generic restaurant template.

## 1. CREATIVE DIRECTION & BRAND IDENTITY

Create a sophisticated visual identity inspired by premium international restaurants, contemporary Mediterranean hospitality and luxury lifestyle brands.

Visual atmosphere:
- Elegant, warm, sophisticated and memorable.
- Cinematic photography.
- Beautiful editorial typography.
- Premium spacing and refined compositions.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Subtle luxury details.

Color palette:
- Deep charcoal: #171717
- Warm ivory: #F5F0E8
- Champagne gold: #C5A46D
- Warm beige: #D8C8AF
- Soft black: #101010

Use gold only as an elegant accent, never excessively.

Typography:
- Elegant serif typography for headlines.
- Modern sans-serif typography for body text.
- Large editorial headings.
- Perfect readability and balanced typography.

## 2. HERO SECTION — FIRST IMPRESSION

Create an exceptional full-screen hero section.

Requirements:
- Large cinematic background photograph of the actual establishment or an appropriate premium hospitality atmosphere.
- Dark gradient overlay for readability.
- Elegant BALKON logo/wordmark.
- Main headline: "MORE THAN A PLACE. AN EXPERIENCE."
- Supporting text: "Discover a unique atmosphere, exceptional moments and unforgettable experiences at Balkon."
- Primary CTA: "Discover Our Menu"
- Secondary CTA: "Reserve Your Table"
- Elegant scroll indicator.
- Smooth entrance animations.

The hero must immediately communicate quality, exclusivity and atmosphere.

## 3. NAVIGATION

Create a sophisticated transparent navbar that becomes a solid dark background when scrolling.

Navigation:
- Home
- About
- Menu
- Gallery
- Experience
- Contact

Include:
- Logo on the left.
- Navigation links in the center.
- Reservation button on the right.
- Mobile hamburger menu with an elegant animated transition.
- Sticky navigation.
- Smooth scrolling.

## 4. ABOUT BALKON

Create an editorial storytelling section.

Headline:
"A PLACE TO CONNECT. A SPACE TO ENJOY."

Present Balkon as a destination where gastronomy, atmosphere and memorable moments come together.

Design:
- Asymmetrical image composition.
- Beautiful restaurant photography.
- Elegant text blocks.
- Subtle decorative elements.
- Scroll-triggered animations.

Do not invent the restaurant's history, awards or achievements. Use editable placeholder content where information is missing.

## 5. MENU EXPERIENCE

Build a visually sophisticated digital menu.

Categories:
- Coffee & Hot Drinks
- Cold Drinks
- Breakfast
- Food & Specialties
- Desserts
- Other categories based on the actual menu.

Requirements:
- Premium food photography.
- Elegant menu cards.
- Category navigation.
- Product name and description.
- Actual prices only when provided.
- Responsive layout.
- Smooth hover interactions.

Add a CTA: "Explore Full Menu".

Menu content must be easy to update.

## 6. GALLERY — VISUAL STORYTELLING

Create an immersive photographic gallery.

Include:
- Interior architecture.
- Outdoor seating.
- Food presentation.
- Drinks.
- Details and decoration.
- Lifestyle and atmosphere.

Use:
- Asymmetrical masonry grid.
- High-quality photography.
- Image hover effects.
- Fullscreen lightbox.
- Smooth transitions.

Use authentic Balkon photographs when available. Never present stock photographs as actual photographs of the establishment.

## 7. EXPERIENCE SECTION

Create an attractive section highlighting the experience of visiting Balkon.

Focus on:
- Atmosphere.
- Food and drinks.
- Comfort.
- Social moments.
- Hospitality.

Use premium imagery and concise editorial copy.

Avoid unsupported claims.

## 8. RESERVATION SYSTEM

Create a professional reservation interface.

Fields:
- Full name.
- Phone number.
- Email (optional).
- Number of guests.
- Reservation date.
- Reservation time.
- Special requests.

Features:
- Form validation.
- Success and error states.
- Mobile-friendly interface.
- WhatsApp reservation option.
- Clear confirmation feedback.

Important: Do not simulate a confirmed reservation. Connect the form to a real backend or reservation service before claiming that a booking is confirmed.

## 9. CONTACT & LOCATION

Create a premium contact section.

Include:
- Establishment name.
- Actual address.
- Phone number.
- WhatsApp button.
- Google Maps integration.
- Opening hours.
- Instagram link.
- Contact form.

Use verified business information only.

## 10. FOOTER

Create a sophisticated footer containing:
- BALKON logo.
- Short brand description.
- Navigation.
- Contact details.
- Social media links.
- Opening hours.
- Copyright.

Use a dark background and refined gold accents.

## 11. ADVANCED ANIMATIONS

Use Framer Motion for:
- Smooth page transitions.
- Scroll reveal animations.
- Staggered text entrances.
- Image reveal effects.
- Elegant hover interactions.
- Navbar transitions.
- Mobile menu animation.
- Subtle parallax effects.

Animations must be smooth, lightweight and purposeful.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion accessibility settings.

Avoid excessive animations that negatively affect performance.

## 12. TECHNICAL STACK

Preferred technologies:
- React + TypeScript.
- Vite.
- Tailwind CSS.
- Framer Motion.
- Lucide React icons.
- React Router if multiple pages are required.

Architecture:
- Reusable components.
- Clean folder structure.
- Maintainable code.
- Reusable design tokens.
- Modular sections.
- Consistent naming conventions.

## 13. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Perfectly optimize for:
- Mobile: 360px–480px.
- Tablet: 768px–1024px.
- Desktop: 1280px–1920px.
- Large displays: 1920px+.

No horizontal overflow.
No broken layouts.
No overlapping text.
No distorted images.

Mobile must feel like a native premium hospitality experience.

## 14. SEO & PERFORMANCE

Implement:
- Semantic HTML5.
- SEO-friendly page titles.
- Meta descriptions.
- Open Graph metadata.
- Local SEO for Balkon, L'Aouina, Tunis.
- Image optimization.
- Lazy loading.
- Accessible alt attributes.
- Sitemap and robots.txt.
- Fast loading.
- Core Web Vitals optimization.

## 15. FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

The website must:
- Look exceptionally professional.
- Have a coherent and recognizable identity.
- Use real and high-quality imagery.
- Have no generic template appearance.
- Contain no broken links.
- Have functional buttons and navigation.
- Have working contact and reservation flows.
- Be fully responsive.
- Be accessible.
- Be optimized for production.

Do not use emojis as design elements.
Do not invent business information.
Do not use random gradients everywhere.
Do not create empty sections.
Do not leave buttons without functionality.
Do not use generic dashboard-style cards.

## 16. DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW

Before coding:
1. Analyze the project structure.
2. Establish the visual design system.
3. Plan all sections.
4. Identify missing business information and assets.

Then:
1. Build the complete frontend.
2. Implement all interactions.
3. Optimize responsiveness.
4. Test every page and button.
5. Fix all errors.
6. Prepare the production build.

Do not stop after creating a hero section or a basic prototype.

Deliver a complete, polished, premium, production-ready BALKON website.

**FINAL CREATIVE GOAL:**
Make visitors feel the atmosphere of Balkon before they even enter the establishment. The website should communicate emotion, elegance, quality and a memorable hospitality experience.
